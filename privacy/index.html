Aevo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Aevo shows your pet's age next to its age in human years, and can show people's ages too. It is built so that your information stays with you. This policy explains what that means.

Who is responsible

Aevo is made by Stanislav Kaspřík, an independent developer in the Czech Republic, who is responsible for the data described here (the “controller” under the EU's General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). You can reach him at go.aevo.support@gmail.com.

Data we collect

Nothing you enter in the app. Aevo has no accounts, no Aevo server and no advertising, and the developer cannot see the names, birth dates or photos you add.

Aevo does not track you: it does not use the advertising identifier, does not link anything it sends to you, and does not combine it with data from other companies' apps or websites.

Unless you turn it off, Aevo sends anonymous usage data: how the app is set up and used, never what you enter in it. It cannot identify you, and it shows the developer which parts of Aevo people use, so they can be improved.

What the app stores, and where

The details you add for each pet or person (name, birth date, species, whether it's a he or a she if you say so, a dog's size and an optional photo), with when it was added, its place in your list and whether its birthday reminder is on, are stored:

  • on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, where Aevo's widgets can also read them (the widgets keep a smaller copy of each photo on the device), and
  • in your own iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on for Aevo, so they stay in sync across your devices. This data is kept in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Aevo only uses the camera or your photo library when you choose to add a photo, and only the photo you pick or take. It keeps the part you frame as a new image, without the original's location or other details. Aevo only adds to your photo library if you choose to save a shared card there.

Your settings in Aevo, such as the appearance, date format and card style, are kept on each device and are not synced.

Birthday reminders, if you turn them on, are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and nothing is sent anywhere to deliver them.

Anonymous usage data

To learn how Aevo is used, the app sends anonymous usage data to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service run by TelemetryDeck GmbH in Germany, whose servers are in the EU. TelemetryDeck's code is the only third-party code in Aevo, and it runs only in the app, never in the widgets.

This is on when you first open Aevo. You can turn it off at any time in Aevo's Settings › Privacy › Share Anonymous Usage Data. From then on nothing is sent from that iPhone, iPad or Mac until you turn it back on. Each device keeps its own setting.

What is sent:

  • that Aevo was opened and when (the hour, weekday and date), whether it is the first launch after installing it, the day it was first opened, and how long and how often it is used;
  • once a day, how the app is set up: how many dogs, cats and people you have added (only the numbers, and from 10 on just “10 or more”), the card style, whether birthday reminders are on and notifications allowed, how many widgets of each kind you have added (from 3 on just “3 or more”) and whether any of them shows a card you have deleted, the app icon, appearance and date format you chose, whether you have Aevo Plus, on which plan, whether it is a free trial or shared with you through Family Sharing, and whether you had it before;
  • how far you got through the welcome screens and how they ended: whether you added a pet from them, skipped them, or your cards arrived from iCloud meanwhile;
  • when the Aevo Plus page is opened and from where (Settings, a widget or app icon that needs Aevo Plus, or the welcome screens), whether its plans could be loaded and a free trial was offered, which plan was bought, whether the purchase went through, is waiting for approval, was cancelled or failed, and what Restore Purchases found;
  • when the Support Aevo page is opened, which of its two designs it showed, whether the tips came into view, which tip size was chosen and whether the purchase went through;
  • details of the device and the app: device model and type, system and app version, whether the app came from the App Store or TestFlight, language, region and time zone, screen size and orientation, and display and accessibility settings such as dark mode, text size, bold text, reduce motion, reduce transparency and increased contrast;
  • an anonymous identifier, so that a device using Aevo every day is counted once. It is made from the identifier iOS gives the developer's apps on your device, scrambled (hashed) on the device and again by TelemetryDeck, so it cannot be turned back into the original or connected to you.

Never sent: the names, birth dates or photos you add, whether a pet or person is a he or a she, anything you type, which pets or people the numbers are about, the text of reminders, prices, currencies, payment details or purchase IDs, your Apple Account, email address or location. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses.

The data is used only to understand and improve Aevo. It is not used for advertising or tracking, not combined with data from other apps or companies, and not sold. TelemetryDeck processes it on the developer's behalf; see the TelemetryDeck Privacy Policy and its privacy FAQ.

Purchases

Aevo Plus and tips are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment under the Apple Privacy Policy, and Aevo never receives your payment details, name or email address.

To unlock Aevo Plus, the app asks the App Store on your device which purchases your Apple Account has, including Aevo Plus shared with you through Family Sharing. It remembers on your device whether Aevo Plus is active, so the widgets can read it, and whether you have left a tip, so it can thank you.

While Share Anonymous Usage Data is on, the anonymous usage data includes which plan or tip was bought, whether the purchase went through, what Restore Purchases found, and whether Aevo Plus is active and on which plan. It never includes the price, your payment details or anything that identifies the purchase or your Apple Account. With the setting off, nothing about your purchases leaves your device, except what Apple needs to handle them.

To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings in Aevo and tap Manage on the plan row at the bottom of the Widgets card, or go to Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. The anonymous usage data goes only to TelemetryDeck, which processes it for the developer.

When you share a card, Aevo hands it to your device's share sheet, and it goes only where you choose to send it: an image of the card (with the name, age and photo if it has one) and a line of text with the name and age, plus a link to Aevo on the App Store. Aevo does not upload it anywhere.

If you use Send Feedback, or email the developer from this policy, the email goes from your own mail account to the developer, with the app and iOS versions added by Send Feedback. It is used only to reply to you.

Some links take you out of Aevo, where that service's own privacy policy applies: the Instagram pages of the developer's dogs on Support Aevo, and Apple's privacy policy. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any website, GitHub receives your IP address when the page is opened, including inside Aevo, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. To remove it:

  • delete a pet or person in the app, which removes it from your devices and your iCloud;
  • delete the app to remove all of its data from that iPhone, iPad or Mac;
  • to remove the iCloud copy, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud, go to your iCloud storage, choose Aevo and delete its data.

There is no Aevo account or server, so the developer holds nothing about you to delete. If you have emailed the developer, ask and those emails will be deleted.

To stop sending anonymous usage data, turn off Settings › Privacy › Share Anonymous Usage Data in Aevo. What was already sent cannot be connected to you, so it cannot be found and deleted for you either; it stays only as anonymous statistics.

Your rights

The anonymous usage data is collected because the developer has a legitimate interest in understanding how Aevo is used and making it better (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning off Share Anonymous Usage Data, which stops it straight away. Emails are used to answer you, for the same reason.

Depending on where you live, you have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. The data you add to Aevo is already in your hands, on your devices and in your iCloud, where you can see, change or delete it. For anything else, email go.aevo.support@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection or the one where you live.

Children

Aevo is for everyone, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Its anonymous usage data is the same for everyone and cannot identify anyone.
