Faunage Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Faunage shows your pet's age next to its age in human years. It is built so that your information stays with you. This policy explains what that means.

Data we collect

None. Faunage has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code. The developer cannot see anything you enter in the app.

What the app stores, and where

The details you add for each pet (name, birth date, species, size and an optional photo) are stored:

  • on your iPhone, where the Faunage widget can also read them, and
  • in your own iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on for Faunage, so they stay in sync across your devices. This data is kept in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Faunage only uses the camera or your photo library when you choose to add a photo of your pet.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. When you share a pet card, the image goes only where you choose to send it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. To remove it:

  • delete a pet in the app, which removes it from your devices and your iCloud;
  • delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone;
  • to remove the iCloud copy, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud, go to your iCloud storage, choose Faunage and delete its data.

Children

Faunage collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
