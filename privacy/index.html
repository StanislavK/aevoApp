Aevo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Aevo shows your pet's age next to its age in human years, and can show people's ages too. It is built so that your information stays with you. This policy explains what that means.

Data we collect

Nothing you enter in the app. Aevo has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer cannot see the names, birth dates or photos you add.

Unless you turn it off, Aevo sends anonymous usage data: how the app is set up and used, never what you enter in it. It cannot identify you, and it shows the developer which parts of Aevo people use, so they can be improved.

What the app stores, and where

The details you add for each pet or person (name, birth date, species, a dog's size and an optional photo) are stored:

  • on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, where the Aevo widget can also read them, and
  • in your own iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on for Aevo, so they stay in sync across your devices. This data is kept in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Aevo only uses the camera or your photo library when you choose to add a photo.

Birthday reminders, if you turn them on, are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and nothing is sent anywhere to deliver them.

Anonymous usage data

To learn how Aevo is used, the app sends anonymous usage data to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service run by TelemetryDeck GmbH in Germany, whose servers are in the EU. TelemetryDeck's code is the only third-party code in Aevo, and it runs only in the app, never in the widgets.

This is on when you first open Aevo. You can turn it off at any time in Aevo's Settings › Privacy › Share Anonymous Usage Data. From then on nothing is sent from that iPhone, iPad or Mac until you turn it back on. Each device keeps its own setting.

What is sent:

  • that Aevo was opened and when, the day it was first opened, and how long and how often it is used;
  • once a day, how the app is set up: how many dogs, cats and people you have added (only the numbers, and from 10 on just “10 or more”), the card style, whether birthday reminders are on and notifications allowed, how many widgets of each kind you have added, the app icon, appearance and date format you chose, and whether you have Aevo Plus and on which plan;
  • how far you got through the welcome screens, and whether you added a pet from them;
  • when the Aevo Plus or Support Aevo page is opened, which plan or tip was bought there, and whether the purchase went through, is waiting for approval, was cancelled or failed;
  • details of the device and the app: device model, system and app version, language, region and time zone, screen size, and display settings such as dark mode, text size, bold text and reduce motion;
  • an anonymous identifier, so that a device using Aevo every day is counted once. It is made from the identifier iOS gives the developer's apps on your device, scrambled (hashed) on the device and again by TelemetryDeck, so it cannot be turned back into the original or connected to you.

Never sent: the names, birth dates or photos you add, anything you type, which pets or people the numbers are about, the text of reminders, prices, currencies, payment details or purchase IDs, your Apple Account, email address or location. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses.

The data is used only to understand and improve Aevo. It is not used for advertising or tracking, not combined with data from other apps or companies, and not sold. TelemetryDeck processes it on the developer's behalf; see the TelemetryDeck Privacy Policy and its privacy FAQ.

Purchases

Aevo Plus and tips are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment under the Apple Privacy Policy, and Aevo never receives your payment details, name or email address.

To unlock Aevo Plus, the app asks the App Store on your device which purchases your Apple Account has, including Aevo Plus shared with you through Family Sharing. It remembers on your device whether Aevo Plus is active, so the widgets can read it, and whether you have left a tip, so it can thank you.

While Share Anonymous Usage Data is on, the anonymous usage data includes which plan or tip was bought, whether the purchase went through, and whether Aevo Plus is active and on which plan. It never includes the price, your payment details or anything that identifies the purchase or your Apple Account. With the setting off, nothing about your purchases leaves your device, except what Apple needs to handle them.

To manage or cancel a subscription on your iPhone or iPad, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. The anonymous usage data goes only to TelemetryDeck, which processes it for the developer. When you share a card, the image goes only where you choose to send it. If you use Send Feedback, the email you write goes to the developer with the app and iOS versions added, and is used only to reply to you.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. To remove it:

  • delete a pet or person in the app, which removes it from your devices and your iCloud;
  • delete the app to remove all of its data from that iPhone, iPad or Mac;
  • to remove the iCloud copy, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud, go to your iCloud storage, choose Aevo and delete its data.

To stop sending anonymous usage data, turn off Settings › Privacy › Share Anonymous Usage Data in Aevo. What was already sent cannot be connected to you, so it cannot be found and deleted for you either; it stays only as anonymous statistics.

Children

Aevo collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Its anonymous usage data is the same for everyone and cannot identify anyone.
