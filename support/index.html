Aevo Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email go.aevo.support@gmail.com, or use Settings › Send Feedback in Aevo, which adds your app and iOS versions for you.

How are the ages calculated?

Open Settings › About › How Ages Are Calculated in Aevo. It explains the numbers and lists the sources. Aevo is for information only and isn't veterinary advice.

How do I add a widget?

Open Settings › Widgets in Aevo for step-by-step instructions. One card's small widget is free; the other widgets need Aevo Plus.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings › Aevo Plus › See Aevo Plus, then tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Aevo, open Settings › Aevo Plus › Manage Subscription, or go to Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Family members who share a plan can't cancel it; the person who bought it can.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through reportaproblem.apple.com.

My cards aren't showing on my other device.

Check that both devices use the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and that Aevo is up to date. Changes can take a moment to arrive.
